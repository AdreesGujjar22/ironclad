Frequently Asked Questions

What commercial flooring services does Ironclad provide in Vancouver, BC?
Ironclad Commercial Floors provides commercial flooring installation, commercial flooring repair, commercial flooring replacement, industrial epoxy flooring, garage epoxy flooring, concrete polishing, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), and carpet tile installation across Vancouver, BC and the Lower Mainland.
How do you minimize downtime for operating commercial businesses in Vancouver?
